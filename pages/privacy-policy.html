Privacy Policy

Last updated August 21, 2026

LuxeFind ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices — in plain language.

Information we collect

  • Email address, only if you voluntarily submit it (for example, through the Style Me waitlist form).
  • Standard analytics and cookies, such as pages visited, browser type, and general location, used to understand how the site is used and improve it.

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect payment information — LuxeFind never processes purchases directly; all "Shop Now" links take you to the retailer's own site.

How we use your information

  • To notify you when a feature you signed up for (like Style Me) becomes available.
  • To understand aggregate site usage and improve LuxeFind's content and design.
  • To respond if you contact us directly.

No sale of personal data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. If LuxeFind enters affiliate partnerships in the future, those partners may use their own standard tracking (such as a cookie or click ID) to attribute a purchase back to LuxeFind, consistent with typical affiliate-network practices — see our Affiliate Disclosure.

Cookies

LuxeFind may use standard analytics cookies to understand site traffic. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Children's privacy

LuxeFind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights & contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've submitted to us at any time. To make a privacy request or ask a question about this policy, contact us at luxefindapp@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally as LuxeFind grows, including as we finalize affiliate program compliance. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

This policy is published, in part, to meet the privacy-disclosure requirements of affiliate networks such as Amazon Associates, Rakuten Advertising, and ShopStyle Collective.
