Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated August 21, 2026

In accordance with the FTC's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials:

LuxeFind may earn a commission on qualifying purchases made through links on this site, at no additional cost to you. We only feature sales and retailers we'd genuinely recommend, based on their reputation, breadth of selection, and the value of the specific promotion.

Where things stand right now

As of the date above, LuxeFind has no active affiliate partnerships. Every outbound "Shop Now" link on this site is a direct, unmonetized link to the retailer's official page — we do not currently earn any commission, referral fee, or other compensation from any retailer listed on LuxeFind.

We're publishing this disclosure now, ahead of applying to affiliate programs, so that our practices are transparent and compliant from day one. If and when an affiliate relationship becomes active for a given retailer, we will update this page and clearly label the relevant links.

How this will work once partnerships are active

  • Affiliate links may use a tracking parameter or redirect so the retailer's affiliate network can attribute a sale back to LuxeFind.
  • Using an affiliate link never changes the price you pay.
  • We will never feature a sale or retailer solely because it pays a commission — recommendations are based on genuine value to the reader first.
