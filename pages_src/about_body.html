About LuxeFind

LuxeFind started as a spreadsheet. I've worked in luxury retail in California for years, and every season I found myself doing the same thing for friends and family: keeping a running mental list of which department store had its Friends & Family sale open, what the gift-card thresholds were that week, and where a specific bag or coat was actually cheapest right now.

Saks, Neiman Marcus, Bergdorf Goodman, and Bloomingdale's each run their own calendar of designer sales, Friends & Family events, and promotional gift-card offers — and the good windows are often short and easy to miss. I built LuxeFind because I wanted one place to see all of it at a glance, without hunting through five different sites and email newsletters.

What LuxeFind is today

Right now, LuxeFind tracks active and upcoming sale events across major luxury department stores, and offers an illustrative price-comparison view for popular categories. Style Me, an AI styling companion, is in development. This is an independent, self-funded side project — not a product of, or endorsed by, any retailer mentioned on this site.

What's next

I'm working on live price syncing and eventually affiliate partnerships with the retailers featured here, which will let LuxeFind earn a small commission on purchases at no extra cost to you — see the Affiliate Disclosure page for details on where that stands today. Until then, every link on this site goes straight to the retailer, unmonetized.
