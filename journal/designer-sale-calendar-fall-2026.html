Designer Sale Calendar: What's Live Right Now (Fall 2026)

Sale calendars shift constantly, so treat this as a snapshot rather than a permanent reference — the live, always-current version lives on the Deals & Sales page. As of late August 2026, here's what's actually open or about to open.

Currently live

Saks Fifth Avenue is running a designer sale through August 31 with up to 70% off ready-to-wear, handbags, and shoes from names like Dior, Fendi, and Balenciaga, alongside a gift-card event (Aug 19-25) offering $50-$900 in gift cards on qualifying spend.

Bergdorf Goodman has select designer styles at up to 70% off plus an additional 25% off select markdowns, running through mid-to-late August, paired with a gift card event offering $100-$1,250 depending on spend.

Neiman Marcus is in one of its longest Friends & Family windows of the year, offering up to 25% off select styles and 20% off fine jewelry through October 3.

Upcoming

Bloomingdale's has a Friends & Family event scheduled for September 18-29, covering designer apparel, handbags, and home at roughly 25% off — worth planning around if you're not in a rush on a specific piece.

How to use this

If you're shopping a specific category — say, fall coats or a handbag — cross-reference which retailer's current window actually includes that category, since exclusion lists vary. And if nothing live fits what you're after, the upcoming Bloomingdale's window in late September is worth waiting for rather than buying at full price now.
